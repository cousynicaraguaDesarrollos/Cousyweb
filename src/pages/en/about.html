About Us

Our Essence

We are a small factory in Nicaragua specializing in personalized and eco-friendly gifts. We craft handmade products using natural canvas, faux leather, and locally sourced leather.

Our mission is to protect the environment while creating meaningful opportunities for people seeking to build a better quality of life for themselves and their families.

Our Founder

Inspiring Story

Our founder, as a child, worked on the streets for almost ten years, an experience that shaped her into a resilient, adventurous, and entrepreneurial woman with a deep passion for building purpose-driven businesses that create a lasting impact on people's lives.

Three years ago, she founded Cousy. Despite operating in a challenging environment, the business continues to grow.
